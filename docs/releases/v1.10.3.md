# DirectorLink v1.10.3

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

A room's or the home's percentage goes to dimmers only, and a member's app stops asking for what it can't see.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## A room's level goes to dimmers only

Since 1.10.2 DirectorLink knows which lights only turn on and off. Now a scene step that sets a percentage for a whole room or the whole home sets it on the dimmers only, and leaves the on/off lights as they are: a door lock or a heater on a light switch is never turned on by "the living room at 50%". The scene editor says so ("Only dimmers get a percentage; switches stay as they are"), and the run's result and History count the switches left as they were, as done, not as a problem. A switch named in the step on its own still turns on, and On, Off and 0 % still reach every light, as before.

## A member's app asks only for what a member sees

A member's app no longer asks the controller for the schedules, which only admins see. Before, it asked after every connection and every minute, and each was refused (a `403` line in DirectorLink's log, and a sealed request through the account each time). Opening Schedules from a kept link now says that schedules are set by the home's admins.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. The cloud is unchanged; no D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.10.2:** nothing is lost; nothing stored changes. A room's level then turns switches on again.

## For developers

- A scene run's result has `on_off_only` (switches a room's or the home's level left as they were), and History's `counts.on_off_only`; the problem `ON_OFF_ONLY`. `GET /v1/system` gains `features.scene_levels_dimmers_only`.
- Releases now start after "Validate DirectorLink" passes for the same commit, without running the tests again (a note under ADR-075).
