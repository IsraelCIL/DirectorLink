# DirectorLink v1.11.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Open the gate from the doorbell's ring, commands that do even more, camera sounds by name, and Control4's own Sonos drivers in Music.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Open the gate from the ring

When a doorbell of DirectorLink's camera agreement rings (the DirectorLink · DoorBird driver, for one), tapping its notification opens that doorbell's own screen: its live picture first, and a big "Open <gate>" button with the usual two taps. DirectorLink finds the gate by itself when the gate's Relay Gate (or Door, or Garage Door) Controller is bound in Composer to the doorbell driver's relay. An admin can add any other door or gate at the doorbell (a KNX relay, for one) on that screen. Home's ring banner has the same buttons. On Android and computers the notification also shows "Open <gate>…", which opens the screen, never the gate itself. Only users who may open that door see its Open, and History says it was opened "from the doorbell". Nothing new reaches DirectorLink's servers: the alert is sealed as before (ADR-078).

## Commands that do even more

Say or type up to five things at once ("kitchen lights off, close the blinds, play music, turn on the porch light and set the living room AC to 23"); if one part isn't clear, nothing runs and the reply says which part. Blinds and fans now go a step at a time: "open the blinds a bit", "close the kitchen shutters a little more" and "raise the blinds by 20%" move a blind 20 points (or the percent you say) from where it is, and "faster", "slower" or "turn the fan up" move a fan one of its own speeds. "Set the living room lights to half", "dim to a quarter" and "brighter to 80%" set a level, and a room's level says that its on/off lights stay as they are. "Warmer" and "cooler" no longer need the AC named when the room has one thermostat ("יותר חם בחדר שינה"); with two, it asks which. In English, Hebrew, Spanish and Italian, still without AI and never on a guess: questions, times, "don't" and "I'm cold" never act, and a door or gate never opens by a step (ADR-079).

## Camera sounds by name

A camera alert now says what the camera heard: a smoke alarm, a CO alarm, a siren, a baby crying, someone talking, a dog barking, a burglar alarm, a car horn or glass breaking ("Smoke alarm at Garden at 21:14."), where 1.10 said only "Alert". A smoke or CO alarm is never held back behind the same camera's other alerts, and it keeps its own notification on the phone. The DirectorLink · UniFi Protect driver sends its sounds as alerts from its next version (ADR-080).

## Control4's Sonos drivers

In a home whose Control4 project also has Control4's own Sonos drivers, their devices no longer appear in each room as "other devices" next to the same speakers in Music, while Sonos is On in Composer. Scripts still find them in `/v1/devices`, marked `part_of_music` (ADR-080).

## Smaller logs on DirectorLink's servers

The relay's log keeps DirectorLink's own lines (a home connecting or dropping, sign-ins, alerts, backups, errors) and no longer a line for every request and message, about 1 % of what it was (ADR-081).

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself.
- **The cloud** gets a Worker update for the logs only (no D1 migration); the app and the driver work with the Worker before or after it.
- **Going back to 1.10.3:** nothing stored is lost. The doors an admin added at a doorbell stay in the controller unread until 1.11 is back; a backup made by 1.11.0 is refused by 1.10.3, as for any newer backup.

## For developers

- `GET /v1/doorbells` and `/{id}`: every doorbell has `doors: [{id, link: "automatic" | "manual", can_open}]`. New `PUT /v1/doorbells/{doorbellId}/doors` (`{"door_ids": [...]}`, admins; up to 10 a doorbell and 50 doorbells, 409 `DOORBELL_LINK_LIMIT_REACHED`). `POST /v1/relays/{id}/pulse` may carry `{"doorbell": id}`: History then has `ids.doorbell_id`. Backups gain the optional `doorbell_doors` section.
- `GET /v1/devices`: every device has `part_of_music` (true for Control4's Sonos drivers while Sonos is On).
- The camera agreement stays version 1. Its labels gain the sounds (`Smoke Alarm`, `CO Alarm`, `Siren`, `Baby Crying`, `Speech`, `Barking`, `Burglar Alarm`, `Car Horn`, `Glass Break`); a driver fires `Alert` for a smoke or CO alarm even while another of its alerts lasts (docs/CAMERA_DRIVERS.md).
- `scripts/dev_server.py --control4-sonos`; with `--agreement-cameras --door-controllers` the doorbell 68 has its gate 76.

## Known issues

- The UniFi Protect driver 1.0.1 sends sounds as its own Sound Detected event only: sound alerts arrive once its next version sends them as `Alert`.
- The ring line on other screens still leads to Home's banner, not to the doorbell's screen.
- On iPhone and iPad the app still reaches the home through DirectorLink's servers, even at home (WebKit, see the README).
