# DirectorLink v1.7.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.7: Samsung refrigerators, doorbell rings and more alerts on your phone (sealed end to end), scene links for your phone's own automations, joining from another device without a link, and DirectorLink in numbers on the website.

## Samsung refrigerators

With the free [DirectorLink · Samsung Refrigerator](https://directorlink.io/drivers/samsung-refrigerator) driver in the project, the refrigerator shows in its room as one device (ADR-049).

- **What you see:** fridge and freezer temperatures and setpoints, whether a door is open, the water filter, and whether the refrigerator is online. Home and the room say when a door is open, and a refrigerator can be a favorite.
- **What you switch:** Power Cool, Power Freeze, Sabbath Mode and the ice maker. A switch moves at once and shows "Turning on…" until the refrigerator confirms through Samsung's cloud, usually within seconds. After 65 seconds without a confirmation the app says so, and still shows one that comes later.
- **Who:** members and admins switch; viewers see the state.
- **In scenes and schedules:** a new Refrigerators action (which refrigerator, which feature, on or off), so a schedule can switch Sabbath Mode or Power Cool.
- **A door left open** (the refrigerator driver's Door Left Open, 5 minutes by default) is listed in History under Doors, and can be an alert (below).

## Alerts for everyone, sealed end to end

Alerts are no longer for admins only, and they can now name things, without DirectorLink's servers learning what they say (ADR-050).

- **Doorbell rings** reach your phone with the app closed: "<doorbell> rang at 18:42". Tapping opens Home with the ring. At most one notification per doorbell in 30 seconds.
- **A refrigerator door left open**, for members and admins: "…has been open for at least 6 min".
- **A door or gate opened**, for admins who turn it on (off by default): which one, and who opened it, also when it was opened in Control4 (its app, a keypad, its programming).
- **A schedule had a problem** now names the schedule's scene. **Home offline** for 10 minutes stays as before.
- **Each device chooses** in **Settings → Controller → Alerts on this device**: a switch for each kind its role may get.
- **How it stays private:** the controller encrypts what each alert says separately for every device that should get it, and every alert has the same size. Our servers only deliver it: they learn which devices get an alert and when, never which doorbell, door, person or schedule. They can still tell a doorbell ring (it is kept only a minute), and that an alert sent to someone who isn't an admin is about a refrigerator. The privacy page has the details.

On iPhone and iPad, alerts need DirectorLink added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later); with 1.7.0 that app can join by itself (below).

## Scene links

Give a scene a private link, and your phone's own automations can run it: an iPhone Shortcuts automation when you arrive home, Siri, an NFC tag, or an Android app such as HTTP Shortcuts, Tasker or MacroDroid (ADR-051).

- **Make one:** open a scene, then **Link for automations** → **Make a link**. The app shows it once, with Copy buttons, a QR code and the steps for iPhone and Android. **Links for automations**, a row on the Scenes list, lists every link.
- **Only for safe scenes:** lights, climate, fans, blinds, music and refrigerators. A scene that opens doors or gates can never have a link, and adding a door or gate to a linked scene removes its link (the app asks first).
- **It never runs by accident:** opening a link in a browser (or a chat app's preview) shows a page with a Run button; only that button, or an automation's request, runs the scene.
- **It ends when it should:** Replace or Remove it at any time. A link stops with the key of the device that made it (revoked, forgotten or expired), and Composer's **Revoke All API Keys** and the new **Remove All Scene Links** end every link. If the house changes hands, the new owner should run Revoke All API Keys.
- **Every run is in History**, by the link's name.
- **Guessing doesn't help:** the link's secret is 160 bits, and someone trying wrong links from one network is stopped after 10 tries in 10 minutes, without blocking the family's runs from elsewhere.
- **What our servers see:** a link is not sealed end to end like everything else, because an automation can't do the encryption. Whoever has the link can run that one scene, from anywhere, until it is replaced or removed. Our servers see the link and its secret when it is used, but never which scene it runs, and they log each run (home, link id, result) for a few days, never the secret. Links need Remote Access on and the home linked to an account.

## Join from another device

iOS gives DirectorLink on the Home Screen its own storage and opens every link in Safari, so an "Add my other device" link never reached it. Now no link is needed (ADR-053):

1. On the new device (for example the Home Screen app, signed in to your account), tap **Join from another device** on the Connect screen.
2. On a device that already reaches the home with an admin key, the request appears. Tap **Show code**, type the six digits the new device shows, and tap **Approve**.
3. The new device joins by itself, in your profile.

A wrong code approves nothing, and three wrong codes decline the request. If you didn't ask, decline it and sign out everywhere (Settings → Account). The invitation travels to the new device encrypted, and the code is built so that nobody in between, our servers included, can fake a match. Devices look for requests once a minute and at once when the app comes to the front.

**Paste invitation link** (on the Connect screen and in Settings → Account) brings a link from Add my other device into the Home Screen app.

## DirectorLink in numbers

The website gets a "DirectorLink in numbers" section: homes linked to an account, people with an account, and driver downloads (ADR-052). Our servers count these totals once an hour and keep only the totals, never anything about one home; your controller sends nothing new. Anyone can read them at `https://api.directorlink.io/v1/stats`. The website shows them once there are 25 homes.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual: no reboot, and keys, scenes, schedules and settings stay. The app updates by itself.
- **Alerts:** each device opens the 1.7.0 app once to keep its alerts and see the new switches. Until then, a device that turned alerts on with 1.6.0 gets only the offline alert.
- **The refrigerator** needs its own driver, [DirectorLink · Samsung Refrigerator](https://directorlink.io/drivers/samsung-refrigerator), set up first.
- **Going back to 1.6.0:**
  - Everything 1.6.0 knows stays.
  - Scene links stop (they answer "not found") until 1.7.0 is back, and work again then.
  - Refrigerator steps are left out of scenes while 1.6.0 runs; 1.7.0 puts them back unless a scene was changed since.
  - Alerts other than offline and schedules stop until 1.7.0 is back.

## For developers

- **New API routes:**
  - `GET /v1/refrigerators`, `GET` and `PATCH /v1/refrigerators/{id}`; the `refrigerators` scene step;
  - `GET` and `PUT /v1/alerts/choices` (each key its own);
  - `GET /v1/scene-links`, and `GET`, `POST`, `DELETE /v1/scenes/{id}/link` (admins).
- **Other API changes:** `GET /v1/system` has `inventory.refrigerators` and `features.refrigerators`, `features.alert_choices` and `features.scene_links`; History has `who.type` `control4` and `link`, `left_open`, and the scene link actions; backups have a `scene_links` section. Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- **Relay protocol:** the hello lists `features` (`scene_links`). New messages: `notify` (sealed parts per key, replacing `alert` from 1.7.0 drivers), `link` and `link_result`.
- **Cloud:**
  - D1 migrations `0008_alert_keys`, `0009_device_requests`, `0010_stats`, applied before the Worker.
  - Push subscriptions carry the key id; every push is padded to 1,024 bytes.
  - `POST /run/{home}.{link}` (and its page on `GET`), `/v1/homes/{id}/device-requests`, public `GET /v1/stats`, and an hourly cron.
  - The 1.7.0 Worker must be deployed before or with the 1.7.0 driver: a 1.6.0 Worker drops `notify`.
- **Website:** directorlink.io/drivers, and its first script (`numbers.js`, `script-src 'self'`, `connect-src https://api.directorlink.io`).
- **CI:** the workflows run on `ubuntu-24.04`.

## Known issues

- **Not yet seen on a real controller or phone:**
  - the refrigerator driver's variables and events as Director gives them to DirectorLink;
  - doorbell ring and door alerts from the DoorBird and the KNX relays (including openings in Control4);
  - scene links from iPhone Shortcuts, Android apps and NFC tags, and link previews in chat apps;
  - joining from the iPhone Home Screen app.
- **Refrigerators with the refrigerator driver 1.0.0:** switches for features the model doesn't have still show, and don't confirm; a door that opens and closes between two of the driver's polls is never seen.
- **Door alerts:** after DirectorLink starts, a KNX relay's first opening in Control4 is missed until the relay has reported its state once.
- **Joining:** only an admin's device can approve; a member's new device needs an invitation from an admin (Paste brings it into the Home Screen app). Both devices need the app open.
- **Scene links:** a scene that turns on all the lights of a room switches everything Control4 calls a light there.
