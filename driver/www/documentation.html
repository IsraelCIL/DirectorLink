DirectorLink

Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

DirectorLink is a free, open-source driver and an app for phones and computers: lights, climate, fans, blinds, cameras, doors and gates, the doorbell, Sonos and more, at home and away, with scenes, schedules, alerts, history and backups. The homeowner makes routine changes in the app; everything DirectorLink automates stays visible here in Composer.

Getting started

  1. DirectorLink shows a Pairing Code in its properties (or run Actions → New Pairing Code). A code lasts 15 minutes and works once.
  2. On a computer or an Android phone at home, open app.directorlink.io, enter the controller's address (or tap Find my controller) and the code. That device gets an admin key.
  3. For use away from home and on iPhone: set Remote Access to On, sign in with Google or Apple in the app and link the home (Settings → Account). Then add phones with Join from another device and invite the family by email.

Settings

  • Door Control: off until doors and gates may be opened from the app. Relay Hold: doors and gates are only pulsed unless allowed.
  • Doors and gates are KNX Contact/Relay devices and Control4's Relay Door, Gate and Garage Door Controllers, whatever relay they drive: DirectorLink sends a controller only its own Open (set its Relay Configuration to Pulse), and shows open or closed when a contact is bound to it. A controller whose Open relay is a KNX Contact/Relay is that relay's door.
  • Remote Access: the app from anywhere, through the homeowner's account, locked end to end.
  • Direct HTTPS: Off by default. Allowed lets the home's owner turn it on in the app (Allowed alone turns nothing on): DirectorLink then also serves its API over HTTPS on port 28443, under a name of the home's own (….dlhome.cc), so that iPhones and iPads reach the controller directly at home. It needs Remote Access and a home linked to an account: the controller makes its own key, and its certificate (Let's Encrypt) comes through DirectorLink's servers, which also keep the name's public DNS record at the controller's LAN address. Back to Off stops it and deletes that record. Direct HTTPS Status shows the name and whether it listens. A router with DNS rebinding protection (OpenWrt, pfSense, FRITZ!Box, some others) hides such a name: the app then simply goes through DirectorLink's servers as before; to allow it, add dlhome.cc to the router's rebind exceptions (OpenWrt: Network → DHCP and DNS, Domain whitelist; pfSense: Services → DNS Resolver, custom option private-domain: "dlhome.cc"; FRITZ!Box: Home Network → Network → Network Settings, DNS rebind protection exceptions).
  • Schedules: On, or Paused to stop every DirectorLink schedule; Schedule Status and Last Automation show what runs and what ran.
  • Alarm Status, Sonos and Jewish Calendar: off by default.
  • Log Level, and Inventory (rooms and devices found).

Actions

  • New Pairing Code, Revoke All API Keys (every key, invitation and scene link), Remove All Scene Links.
  • Print Schedules and Scenes (to the Lua tab), Refresh Project (read the project again).
  • Reset Remote Identity: a last resort; the homeowner links the home again.

Updating

Download DirectorLink.c4z from github.directorlink.io/releases/latest and use Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent. Keep the file name exactly DirectorLink.c4z: a browser's DirectorLink (1).c4z copy installs as a separate driver. Keys, scenes, schedules and settings stay.
