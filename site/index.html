Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.

DirectorLink is a free, open-source driver for Control4 and an app for phones and computers. Control lights, AC, blinds, music, cameras and gates at home and away. Make one-tap scenes and run them by the clock, the sun, the weather, or when you arrive home. Get the doorbell and other alerts on your phone. You make the changes yourself, and your installer still sees everything in Composer. In English, Hebrew, Spanish and Italian.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the driver

    Add DirectorLink to the Control4 project in Composer Pro. It finds the rooms and devices by itself and loads without a reboot.

  2. 2

    Pair at home

    On a computer at home, open the app and enter the controller's address (or tap Find my controller) and the 8-digit pairing code from Composer (DirectorLink → Actions → New Pairing Code). The installer can read it out for you. A code lasts 15 minutes and works once.

  3. 3

    Use it anywhere

    Turn on Remote Access in Composer, sign in with Google or Apple, and link the home. Add your phone with Join from another device, and invite the family by email.

Needs a Control4 controller with OS 3.3.0 or newer. DirectorLink is used every day in a real home: tell us what works on GitHub.

What you get

  • At home and away

    At home the app talks to the controller directly, locked with its own key. Away, and on iPhone, it goes through your account (Google or Apple), locked end to end: our servers pass it along but can't read it.

  • Every device in one app

    Lights and dimmers, AC and floor heating, fans, blinds, cameras, doors and gates, the doorbell, and the alarm's status (read only). Samsung refrigerators with the free DirectorLink driver.

  • Music

    Your Sonos rooms in the app: what's playing, play, pause, skip, volume and your Sonos favorites. DirectorLink talks to the speakers on your home network.

  • Scenes

    One tap for lights, AC, blinds, music and gates. Pick single lights, or copy the house as it is right now. Anything that opens a door asks for a second tap.

  • Schedules

    By the clock, at sunrise or sunset, or when it gets hot, windy or rainy (weather from Open-Meteo). The controller runs them on its own.

  • When you arrive home

    A scene can have a private link for your phone's own automations: iPhone Shortcuts, Android apps, Siri or an NFC tag. Never for doors or gates. How to set it up.

  • Alerts on your phone

    The doorbell, a person, a car or a package at a camera of DirectorLink Drivers (Hikvision today), a door or gate opened and by whom, the fridge door left open, the home offline, a schedule that had a problem. Each device picks its own, and what an alert says is locked for that device.

  • History

    What ran, what didn't and why, who opened which door, and what changed in Composer, for the last 30 days. For admins.

  • Backups

    Everything DirectorLink keeps, in a file locked with your password, or every night to your account, locked with a password only you know.

  • The family, with the right access

    Admins, and members with the rooms, devices and scenes you choose: one set of permissions for each user, on up to five devices. Each user keeps their own language, theme and favorites. A new phone joins with a code from a device you already use.

  • Visible to your installer

    Composer shows what DirectorLink automates and what it ran last, and one switch pauses all its schedules.

  • A real API

    Rooms, lights, climate, blinds, cameras, doors, music, scenes and schedules as REST resources, described by OpenAPI 3.1, with a key and a role for every app.

Arrive home, automatically

Your phone already knows when you get home. Let it run a scene: lights on, AC on, blinds up. Your location stays on your phone; DirectorLink only gets the run.

  1. 1

    Make a link

    In the app, open a scene without doors or gates, such as Arriving Home, then Link for automations → Make a link. Copy the address and the secret.

  2. 2

    iPhone: Shortcuts

    Automation → New → Arrive, pick your home, Run Immediately. Add Get Contents of URL with the address: Method POST, Request Body JSON, a Text field named secret holding the secret.

  3. 3

    Android

    In MacroDroid, Tasker or HTTP Shortcuts, a location (geofence) trigger with an HTTP request: POST to the address, with the secret as the body.

Do the same with Leave for a Leaving Home scene, or with an NFC tag by the door. It needs Remote Access and the home linked to your account. Every run is in History, and Replace or Remove ends a link at once, for example when a phone is lost.

By voice: name a shortcut like the scene and say “Hey Siri, Good night”. For the gate, a link that asks: your phone shows “Open the main gate?”, and only your tap opens it.

DirectorLink Drivers

Free, open-source Control4 drivers for other devices, made by DirectorLink. Each works on its own: Samsung refrigerators, Hikvision and UniFi Protect cameras, and DoorBird doorbells so far.
