Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.

DirectorLink is a free, open-source driver for Control4 and an app for phones and computers. Control lights, AC, blinds, cameras and gates at home and away, make one-tap scenes, and schedule them by the clock, the sun or the weather. You make the changes yourself, and your installer still sees everything in Composer. In English and Hebrew.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the driver

    Add DirectorLink to the Control4 project in Composer Pro. It finds the rooms and devices by itself and loads without a reboot.

  2. 2

    Pair at home

    On a computer at home, open the app and enter the controller's address (or tap Find my controller) and the 8-digit pairing code from Composer (DirectorLink → Actions → New Pairing Code). The installer can read it out for you. A code lasts 15 minutes and works once.

  3. 3

    Use it anywhere

    Turn on Remote Access in Composer, sign in with Google and link the home. Add your phone with Add my other device and invite the family by email.

Needs a Control4 controller with OS 3.3.0 or newer. DirectorLink 1.0 is used every day in one home so far: tell us what works on GitHub.

What you get

  • At home and away

    At home the app talks to the controller directly, locked with its own key. Away, and on iPhone, it goes through your account, locked end to end: our servers pass it along but can't read it.

  • Scenes

    One tap for lights, AC, blinds and gates. Pick single lights, or copy the house as it is right now.

  • Schedules

    By the clock, at sunrise or sunset, or when it gets hot, windy or rainy (weather from Open-Meteo). The controller runs them on its own.

  • The family, with the right access

    View only, control, doors, or admin, for every person and device. Each person keeps their own language, theme and favorites.

  • Visible to your installer

    Composer shows what DirectorLink automates and what it ran last, and one switch pauses all its schedules.

  • A real API

    Rooms, lights, climate, blinds, cameras, doors, scenes and schedules as REST resources, described by OpenAPI 3.1, with a key and a role for every app.
